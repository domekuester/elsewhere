# Editorial Accessibility

Accessibility and ambitious editorial design must coexist.

Inspect semantics and rendered behavior. Check landmarks and headings, meaningful alt architecture, decorative-image silence, reading order, visible focus, full keyboard navigation, Escape/focus return, contrast, caption readability, 44px-class touch targets where practical, zoom/reflow, and screen-reader noise.

Test reduced motion with a complete composed page: no hidden content, blocked access, scroll ownership, or required animation. Preserve quiet interaction and photographic hierarchy; do not solve accessibility by flattening every surface into generic components. Report concrete barriers with route, viewport, input method, and consequence.
