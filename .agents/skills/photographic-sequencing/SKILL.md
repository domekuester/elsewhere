# Photographic Sequencing

Review approved placement without silently recuring it.

1. Confirm publication eligibility and owner exclusions before discussing any frame.
2. Record the authoritative order; do not change it without explicit permission.
3. Review rhythm across scale, orientation, tone, subject, distance, gesture, and narrative relationship.
4. Flag repeated subjects or tonal beats, weak transitions, abrupt pacing, and frames that deserve more space.
5. Recommend layout or scale treatments first. Label any possible reorder as an owner decision, never an automatic edit.

For Journey, preserve approved narrative and photographs. Do not turn chapters into blog posts, timelines, cards, accordions, maps, or travel guides. Never introduce rejected, private, unapproved, generated, or stock imagery.
