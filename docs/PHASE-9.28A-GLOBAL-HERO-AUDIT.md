## Findings

**The two Archive surfaces were the real gap.** They are the deepest photographic rooms on the site —
517 and 48 frames — and both opened on a cream page with a headline and a filter bar. Nothing else on
the site asks a visitor to enter a body of work through typography alone. Both were given a photographic
opening in this phase.

**People was left alone, deliberately.** Its split composition is authored work that the phase brief
explicitly protects, and it already opens on a photograph at full height. Replacing it with a full-bleed
band would flatten a more interesting layout into the same shape as everything else. The new
`Mobile Fotos People` folder deepens the People *pool* (`peopleCandidate` now 88, up from 82) without
touching that composition.

**Black & White curated already had a flagship hero** and keeps it. Its archive counterpart was
deliberately given a *different* monochrome frame — a raised hand against the sun rather than a second
seascape — so the two monochrome rooms do not open identically.

**The Destinations index was assessed and left as type-plus-cards.** It is a list of chapters, and its
four cards are already large photographs. Putting a full-bleed hero above them would show a big
photograph, then immediately four more big photographs, and push the actual chapters below the fold.
The brief warns against putting giant heroes on everything; this is where that applies.

## Method

Same as Phase 9.27, and the incumbents were treated as champions. All 54 publication-eligible frames
that the edit already trusts (featured, or in an `anchor` / `editorial` / `detail` role, at ≥2500px on
the short edge), plus every new frame from the owner folders, were reviewed as contact sheets. Finalists
were rendered in the real band at 1440 and 390 before selection was fixed — which changed the Archive
choice, see the shortlist.

No Phase 9.27 destination hero was replaced. New photography was tested against each incumbent and none
of it came close: the strongest new frames are good archive photographs, not better openings than a
Dotonbori canal, an ochre wall with gulls, a volcanic cirque, or a bridge pylon in blue.
