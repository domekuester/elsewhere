# ELSEWHERE Destination system

Destinations are geographic/editorial objects separate from journeys. Confirmed primary destinations are Malaysia, Thailand, Laos, Vietnam → Phu Quoc, Japan, and France → La Réunion. Public labels foreground Phu Quoc and La Réunion rather than administrative hierarchy.

Publishing threshold: confirmed geography, at least five photographs, a strong approved opening image, visual variety, an intentional manual sequence, safe metadata, and a natural next path. Photo count alone never publishes a page.

Japan is the first destination to meet the threshold: 212 confirmed frames and five already edited photographs spanning Urban, Ocean, Detail, Black & White and People. La Réunion is closest next: 146 confirmed frames and three strong edited anchors, including the current homepage hero, but it needs a broader manual edit before publication.

Malaysia and Thailand aggregate all their journey periods on destination surfaces while journey pages preserve separate visits. Phu Quoc has 21 confirmed frames and one selected monochrome anchor. Laos has one confirmed frame and therefore no public page.
