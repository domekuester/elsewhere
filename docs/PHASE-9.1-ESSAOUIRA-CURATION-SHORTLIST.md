## 1. The hero — confirm or swap

**Selected: P1330989** (photo-0609) — gulls on an ochre parapet, one shadow thrown hard onto the wall.

Chosen because it is landscape, its shadowed lower-left holds the ESSAOUIRA title cleanly, and the
subject is the shadow rather than the scenery. Live now at `/destinations/essaouira/`.

**Three real alternatives**, each with a reason it lost:

| Frame | Strength | Why it is not the hero |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **P1350244** — courtyard open to cloud, one bird | The most distinctive photograph in the whole set | Symmetrical and centred, so the title fights it, and it reads as an interior at the point where the chapter should establish a coastal town. Used instead as the chapter's centre, where it is the strongest moment. |
| **P1340602** — bicycle against the orange door, cat beneath | Richest colour and texture in the set | Too busy behind a title. Kept in the sequence at full strength. |
| **P1330816** — a man and two camels in backlit haze | Beautiful light, genuinely observed | Leading a Morocco chapter with camels is the touristic choice, and it is portrait, which suits a full-bleed opening less well. Kept late in the sequence where the light does its work. |

**If you want to swap:** it is one line — change which Essaouira entry sits first in
`src/data/editorial-selection.ts` with role `anchor`.
