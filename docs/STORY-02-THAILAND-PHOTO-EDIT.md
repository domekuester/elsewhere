**Two of them are replacements, not additions.** `Koh Chang 01.jpg` and `Phayam 01.jpg` have the same
subjects and the same capture dates as `photo-0676` and `photo-0677`, whose source masters have since
disappeared from the folder. The owner replaced the originals with his own edits. The story uses the
new ones.

No creative grade was applied to any of the seven. All sRGB; resize and encode only.

Phase 11.1 recuts the Koh Phayam passage as two distinct views: `photo-0698` opens the island with
the red sun and lone figure; `photo-0700` follows the bungalow sentence as the lived-in view from
where they stayed. The generic second sunset, `photo-0064`, became redundant and lost its Story
reference only. Its source and archive record remain untouched. No creative grade was applied to
`Phayam 02.jpg`; the owner's edit remains the visual source of truth.

Phase 11.2 adds `photo-0701` after the day-rhythm paragraph and before the section's final sentence.
Nothing stronger was removed: the red-sun opener, the bungalow view and the surfer now carry three
different jobs — arrival, living there and beach rhythm. The frame includes anonymous beachgoers;
the owner's explicit Story selection is recorded in the people-review layer without naming anyone.

## People remain tightly controlled

Eleven earlier Thailand frames containing identifiable people remain `storyCandidate: false`; the
build refuses them. `photo-0701` is the single exception because the owner explicitly selected it for
this Koh Phayam passage. The people remain anonymous and no identity is inferred.

The story names Arty, Dori, Pinong, Max, Jam, A, Sakai and Wilmer, and shows none of those named
people. Nothing was substituted for them: the anonymous beach frame describes Koh Phayam's daily
rhythm and does not stand in for anyone in the Tonsai circle.

## Excluded on the owner's instruction

The elephant-riding photographs from Ayutthaya. Out of the story, out of the hero candidates, out of
the social image, out of every selection sheet.
