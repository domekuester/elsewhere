# ELSEWHERE mobile art direction

Mobile remains vertical, photographic and calm rather than becoming a compressed desktop grid.

## Implemented archive decisions

- At 390px and below, the contact sheet becomes an asymmetric single-column sequence.
- Portrait frames occupy 88% width and alternate alignment; landscapes may use the full viewport width.
- Filter controls expose only confirmed visual worlds; secondary year/orientation controls recede on small screens.
- The viewer uses edge-to-edge vertical space with controls moved below the photograph and swipe plus keyboard support.
- Captions remain readable and the ultramarine frame index stays visible.

## Focal architecture

Curation assignments include desktop and mobile focal coordinates. They default to 50/50 but are explicit editorial fields, not destructive crops. These values should be manually reviewed for faces, eyes, hands and horizons before newly selected work becomes a hero.

## Mobile quality gate

Review the first three Home screens, People, Black & White, Archive opening rhythm, filter reachability, viewer portrait/landscape behavior, mobile menu, touch targets, and reduced motion at 390px and 320px before publication.
