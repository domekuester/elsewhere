# Phase 6 — Monetization readiness

ELSEWHERE is structurally ready for future value without displaying commercial UI.

- Photo objects already support role, visibility, relationships, and print-candidate state; future editions can add approved title, paper, size, edition, price, and availability without turning Archive into a shop.
- Destinations, Journeys, Stories, People, Collections, and Field Notes are separate objects, allowing transparent sponsorship or affiliate disclosure at the content-object level.
- The analytics event layer reserves `print_interest_click` and `newsletter_click` without collecting data today.
- Editorial continuations provide future low-pressure placement for licensing, print, book, or partnership inquiries.

Recommended order: professional inquiry/contact, licensing requests, limited prints/books, newsletter, then carefully selected partnerships. Affiliate recommendations and sponsorship should appear only where first-hand editorial knowledge exists and must be visibly disclosed. Display advertising is not recommended for the first launch.

No fake products, prices, availability, partners, or contact details have been created.
