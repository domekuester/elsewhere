# Phase 8.3 — Before / after

## Typography

**Before:** The intended Avenir Next Condensed/Baskerville identity depended on Apple system fonts; Windows and Android could receive Arial/Georgia approximations.

**After:** Barlow Condensed and Literata Regular/Italic are self-hosted as 143KB of WOFF2 with exact OFL notices, true italic, two critical preloads, and zero external font requests.

## Home hero

**Before:** Option C's untuned mobile proof left “it.” on an isolated fourth line.

**After:** The approved relationship remains intact and the editorial line resolves as “as I / remember it.” at 430, 390, and 320.

## Destinations

**Before:** Home used serif destination names while the destination index used condensed display names.

**After:** Literata carries destination names on both surfaces; the display heading remains Barlow Condensed.

## Archive and viewer labels

**Before:** CSS capitalization could render “Black And White,” and small labels/counters were less controlled.

**After:** Authored casing is preserved, captions are slightly more legible, and numeric counters use tabular figures.

## Interaction craft

**Before:** CTA arrow movement responded to pointer hover only; public selects relied on browser-default focus; viewer controls had minimal hover feedback.

**After:** Keyboard focus receives the same restrained directional feedback, selects use the ultramarine focus system, and viewer controls respond quietly without geometry changes.

## Photography

**Before:** Phase 8.25 had already reduced harmful prominent repetition to zero and introduced eleven new Japan photographs.

**After:** Those edits remain unchanged after the final photographic master pass; no unnecessary reshuffle was made.

## Protected systems

**Before:** Home hero, People spread, Black & White edit, viewer geometry, Japan structure, Archive honesty, ultramarine, and native scrolling were protected.

**After:** All remain protected and verified.
