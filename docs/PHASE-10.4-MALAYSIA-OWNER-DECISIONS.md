## 1. Is this beach Pulau Kapas?

**What you need to decide:** whether the island photographs in the story are Kapas.

**Why it matters:** the story says *Pulau Kapas*, *LongSha Campsite*, *Marang* and *Terengganu*
because you told me so, and I trust that completely. But no photograph in the archive has a place
recorded on it. So right now the words name the island and the pictures name nothing.

**The good news:** you do not have to identify nine photographs. Nine of them are the same beach,
photographed over ten days between 23 April and 2 May 2025 — the same palms, the same sand, the same
headland, the same jetty. If one is Kapas, they all are. So this is one question, not nine.

friends, communities, cooking, and people inviting you to eat — and shows nobody. That is a real

to than being one of twenty-two photographs in a piece of writing. The build physically refuses to
render them until you say otherwise.
