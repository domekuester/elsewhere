each chapter was measured at 1440 and 390 for frame scale, page length, overflow and reveal state,

## Japan — re-cut

Japan was the most serious editorial failure in the publication, and it was invisible from the code.

Its chapter held eleven photographs. **Every single one already appeared on Home or on the
Black & White page.** Five were Home's own frames — the Ocean chapter's full-bleed image was also
Japan's chapter hero — and the remaining six were the Black & White collection in order. The
deepest archive in ELSEWHERE, 204 public photographs, revealed nothing whatsoever to a visitor who
had scrolled the homepage.

The chapter is now seventeen frames, **none of which appears anywhere else on the site**. A
constant in `apply-chapters` fails the edit if one ever does.

Sequence: city · alley · lane · light · stone · corridor · gate · bamboo · the island · ridge ·
boat · shore · festival · lighthouse · sea stack · monochrome close.

The hero moved twice. The first candidate (a back street with the sun bursting between buildings)
was strong at contact-sheet size and murky at full-bleed 100svh, where its lens flare read as a
fault rather than as weather. It is now the second frame, where it works. The hero is the Dōtonbori
canal at dusk: depth, warmth, a dark canal band that holds the title, and unmistakably Japan
without being a photograph of a temple.

## La Réunion — published

**114 confirmed photographs, four of Home's most prominent images — including the site hero — and
no way in.** A visitor could see the best photograph on ELSEWHERE and had no page to go to.
It sat in the destinations index as a row of unclickable text.

Its 14-frame chapter uses none of the four frames Home already spends. Sequence: interior · height ·
descent · **river** · forest · human trace · settlement · monochrome · abandonment · **sea** ·
force · quiet · **close**.

## Malaysia — the reason it is not a chapter

Malaysia held 88 public photographs, which by volume made it the third-deepest destination. A
frame-by-frame review found that roughly half of them were one hostel: dormitory signs, the lounge
sofa, the grounds, and a long sequence of a friends' beach boxing session with the same few people.
Fifty-one Malaysian frames are now on editorial hold.

53 remain, and they contain genuinely good work — Kuala Lumpur at night, street food, the clock
tower, storm light over the strait. It is not enough for a chapter with a spine, and volume alone
was the only argument it ever had. It stays at archive depth, reachable and honestly counted.

## Laos — three photographs

Three, and one of them is `bw-market-woman`, which carries Home, People and Black & White. It is
listed with its true count of three and links to those three in the archive. It is not dressed up
as a destination chapter and it is not hidden.

## The quality floor

No public surface is dramatically weaker than the strongest. The mechanism that guarantees this is
that a destination becomes a chapter only when it has a sequence worth walking; otherwise it is
archive depth with a truthful count and a working link. Nothing is deleted, nothing is announced as
"coming soon", and nothing weak is promoted for the sake of completeness.
