## 4. Keiji-san

Research supports the identity: `金子啓次` (Keiji Kaneko) is publicly documented as the artisan who
hand-builds the Shukunegi tarai-bune from cedar and bamboo, described in Japanese coverage as a rare
craftsman rather than a common one. The Story publishes **first name and honorific only**, and uses
"one of the few people who still builds these boats by hand" rather than your stronger "one of the
last", which the sources do not quite carry.

**Question:** two parts. (a) Is "Keiji-san" the spelling you want kept? (b) Are you content that his
surname stays out, given it is publicly available? First name only is the current default.
