# Essaouira — five questions

The chapter is live and does not need these answers. But right now it is carried entirely by the
photographs, with one factual line of provenance. A few sentences from you would let it carry a voice
as well — and would unlock a Field Note, which is the search-facing layer built in Phase 9.

Nothing here has been invented in the meantime. There is no imagined memory anywhere on the site.

**1. When were you there, and why that week?**
The photographs say 8–14 January 2026. Anything about how the trip came about?

**2. What do you remember most?**
Not the sights — the thing that actually stayed.

**3. What kept pulling the camera up?**
Looking at the set: walls meeting sky, birds, shadows, cats, the light at the end of the day. Was
that deliberate, or only visible afterwards?

**4. What did the town sound or feel like?**
Wind, gulls, temperature, the pace of the streets.

**5. Was there one encounter or moment that stayed?**
The man with the camels, the woman who looked back, the surfers at the end of the day — or something
not photographed at all.

**Where answers would go:** the chapter's opening paragraph at `/destinations/essaouira/`, and a first
Field Note. Two or three honest sentences are worth more than a page of description.
