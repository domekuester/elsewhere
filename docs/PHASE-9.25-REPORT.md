# Phase 9.25 — Düsseldorf

A fourth chapter entered a finished publication without disturbing it. 22 photographs became 11 in
a chapter and 22 in the archive, and every one of the 469 photographs Phase 9.2 published came
through unaltered.

## What Düsseldorf turned out to be

Not a city guide, and not a landmark set. Reviewing all 22 as photography before touching a layout
showed one idea repeated with unusual consistency: **a hard-edged form cut against open sky.** A
bridge pylon and its cables. A tower shaft. The curve of a pale wall. Church domes. Bare branches.
Geese. The camera is nearly always tilted up, and the sky is nearly always the second subject.

The files also settled the most important editorial question before it could be guessed at. Ten
separate days across nine months, September 2025 to June 2026. This is not a trip. It is a place
returned to — and that fact, which is *in the photographs*, is the one thing this chapter has that
no other chapter in ELSEWHERE has. It carries no journey, and the page says "Photographed across
9 months" instead of inventing a visit.

## The chapter

Eleven frames, deliberately the shortest in the publication, because 22 photographs do not support
more and padding would be visible.

It opens on the pylon — the idea at full volume, and the boldest hero on the site. Then down to the
ground and a path between plane trees, up into the built city, a small lit window in the tower deck,
the pale curved wall given the anchor, domes, a rotunda with a bird and a small moon, the tower as a
line, and then the bridge again — the same bridge as the hero, seen from across the water at dusk.
A quiet small frame of stepping walls, and out into open sky on geese, full width.

It begins in a tree tunnel on the ground and ends with nothing man-made in the frame.

Six of the 22 frames contain the television tower. Two are in the chapter, well apart, and neither
opens nor closes it. The three weakest frames in the archive are all landmark-plus-lens-flare
pictures and none of them is in the chapter.

## Two edits made only because the page was rendered

The first cut opened with both plane-tree avenues side by side. On the page they read as one
photograph printed twice. One stayed.

The spruce-needle frame survived two edits as a "breath" and, rendered, sat orphaned in a wide empty
band — the only frame in the chapter with no sky in it. Cutting it tightened the opening and closed
the page's worst gap.

Neither was visible in the selection. Both were obvious on the page.

## Baseline

**PRESERVED.** Zero pre-existing photographs altered, zero removed. Home hero, Home composition,
typography, ultramarine, native scroll, Studio, Licensing, the Black & White curated edit and the
People page are all untouched. Japan, La Réunion and Essaouira measure identically to their
Phase 9.2 state at all ten viewports.

Three Phase 9.2 surfaces changed, each because a fourth chapter demanded it:

- The destinations index moved to two columns. A fourth card had turned auto-fit into three plus an
  orphan; two columns fills every row, roughly doubles each photograph, and makes the markup's own
  `sizes="48vw"` true for the first time.
- The provenance line can express a long span, because "276 days in September" is absurd.
- A chapter that spans months and belongs to no journey no longer calls itself "travel photography",
  because that would be false. The other three keep their exact Phase 9.2 titles.

## Two latent defects in the baseline, found and fixed

**The derivative generator did not know about `editorial-hold`.** It still used the pre-9.2 withheld
list, so regenerating derivatives — which this phase had to do — would have silently rebuilt public
images for all 63 photographs Phase 9.2 withheld. Caught before running; verified afterwards at 63
held frames and 0 resurrected derivatives.

**La Réunion had no social card.** Its Open Graph image fell back to an uncropped derivative. It now
has a real 1200×630 card, as Düsseldorf does.

Neither was reachable without a new import. Growth is what exposed them, which is the argument for
growing carefully.

## Compared with the other chapters

**Stronger in:** hero boldness — the pylon is the most graphic opening on the site; colour identity,
being the only chapter built on cold sky; formal consistency, since every frame shares one idea;
and the structural rhyme of opening and closing on the same bridge.

**Weaker in:** depth, at 22 photographs against Japan's 204 and La Réunion's 101; range of subject,
being almost entirely architecture and sky; human presence, having none at all where Japan has
fishermen and performers; monochrome, having none; and written voice, having none — though Essaouira
and La Réunion share that gap.

**Equal in:** sequencing craft, scale rhythm (30–70% at desktop, 59–100% at mobile — identical to
the strongest), image fidelity, viewer integration, typography, accessibility, technical
correctness and brand fit.

Düsseldorf is the shortest chapter and does not pretend otherwise. It earns its place on
concentration rather than volume, which is the correct argument for a 22-photograph archive.

contains. A single strong human frame would move that number more than anything design can do.

landmark repeated six times — from which a genuinely strong eleven were available.
