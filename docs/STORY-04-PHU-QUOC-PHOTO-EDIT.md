## Sequence rationale

The edit begins cooler and more controlled than Laos. Monumental architecture and repeated design details establish the owner’s specific environment without making the whole island a claim. The sunset is allowed to be attractive but sits inside the fireworks passage rather than pretending to overturn the verdict. Fishing boats introduce a lived island counterpoint without becoming a postcard, and the clock closes the recurring seven-o’clock motif before the personal departure and blunt final line.
