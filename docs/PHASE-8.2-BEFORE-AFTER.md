# Phase 8.2 — Before / after

## Japan

**Before:** Ten sequence frames were present, but only frames 1–4 had authored desktop placement. Frames 5–10 collapsed into grid-track-sized thumbnails.

**After:** A repeating, orientation-aware six-role sequence supports variable chapter length. All ten Japan sequence frames remain editorially scaled from 1600px to 320px. No photograph was replaced or re-cropped.

## Archive

**Before:** 23 of 500 public photographs were world-classified, while six world filters exposed result sets as small as one photograph.

**After:** World filters require an editorial minimum of eight reviewed assignments. Visible filters are `All` and `Black and white` (10). Year, frame, place, and random discovery remain available. Newly reviewed collections reappear automatically when they reach the threshold.

## Copy

**Before:** Public pages contained “Working title,” story-development placeholders, future-collection roadmap entries, “in edit,” “editorial review,” and similar production language.

**After:** Those phrases are absent from public source and built output. Necessary travel-timeline uncertainty remains factual.

## Viewer and accessibility

**Before:** Closing the viewer attempted to focus an unfocusable image span. Archive controls were named only by frame number, and 478 images repeated one generic alt sentence.

**After:** Focus returns to the initiating button while animation geometry remains tied to the image. Controls use factual, content-aware labels. Unreviewed image alts are empty rather than fabricated or repetitive, and the catalog records explicit review status. The confirmed lighthouse description is corrected.

## Responsive imagery

**Before:** Catalog images used one fixed source without `srcset` or `sizes`.

**After:** Archive and destination images expose role-aware responsive sources using existing 960, 1800, and 3200 long-edge derivatives with accurate width descriptors.

## Smaller defects

**Before:** The desktop Scroll arrow inherited vertical writing mode; the People heading width cap fought its authored line break; World 04 retained a negative offset through the 980–1100 range; viewer counts used two-digit padding.

**After:** The arrow points down independently, the People heading preserves two authored lines, the intermediate-width offset is neutralized, and viewer counts use three digits.

## Deferred

Photographic repetition, Japan hero art direction and sequence re-edit, typography portability implementation, Black & White density, the second mountain beat, the Jungle photograph, People expansion, and mobile header treatment remain owner-gated.
