# Phase 9.25 — Düsseldorf shortlist

Everything on this page is a decision already made and already live. Nothing here blocks anything.
Read it only if you want to change something.

## Selected hero

**P1350974** — red cables running down a white bridge pylon into deep blue sky.

It is the strongest single photograph in the set, it states the chapter's idea (a hard form against
open sky) at full volume, and it gives Düsseldorf a colour no other chapter has.

### Three real alternatives

| Frame | Why it could win | Why it did not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **P1320755** pale curved wall across cloud | The most distinctive, most authored frame in the archive | Too quiet to carry a full-screen opening, and its pale lower edge is where the title sits. It is the chapter's anchor instead |
| **P1310315** tower shaft into teal | Powerful vertical, unmistakably the city | It is the landmark. Opening on it would make the chapter about the tower |
| **P1320572** glass rotunda, bird, small moon | Layered and quietly strange; excellent title area | Less immediate; better as a mid-sequence beat, where it now sits |

## The chapter, in order

## Weakest three

Public in the archive, but they would lower the chapter's level. Say the word if you disagree.

- **P1320253** tower over a river sunset — proof of location, with flare
- **P1360018** tower through branches — the lens flare is the picture, and it is an accident
- **P1320271** low sun between bare trees — the same flare problem

## Black & White

**None.** Measured, not guessed: per-pixel channel spread on all 22 frames reads 66–222 where a true
greyscale conversion reads 0. There is no monochrome work in this archive.

## People

**None.** Nobody is the subject of any frame. A distant figure on a promenade does not make a People
photograph.

## Near-duplicate groups

**None.** No bursts, no crops, no colour/mono variants — the 22 frames are 22 distinct photographs.
