# Flagship Pass 3 Audit

## Finding

The second pass established a memorable visual language, but the platform still behaved primarily as one long homepage. People was emotionally promising but depended on one repeated portrait, Black & White had no authored pathway, and hundreds of photographs had no clear editorial route into the publication.

## Strengths preserved

- Mountain hero and personal opening statement.
- Alternating Obsidian and Gallery Ivory chapters.
- Ultramarine memory mark and asymmetric visual-world index.
- Honest separation of visual classification and geography.
- Minimal Astro/HTML architecture, responsive derivatives, lazy loading, and reduced-motion behavior.
- Text-led destination index rather than booking-style country cards.

## Weaknesses addressed

- `P1210572.jpg` appeared twice and made People feel like one individual rather than a publishing system.
- There was no page-level depth beyond destinations.
- The closing archive implied scale but did not demonstrate how curated depth would work.
- Monochrome photographs existed in the archive without an artistic edit.
- Photograph metadata lacked explicit editorial role, emotional intensity, monochrome, people-presence, and rhythm fields.
- Main navigation pointed People to a homepage fragment instead of a real editorial surface.

## Editorial decisions

`P1260248.jpg` replaces the rejected portrait because its meaning comes from action and attention rather than posed beauty. `P1260635.jpg` becomes the People world representative because it shows a worker within a social space. Black & White is treated as a collection with its own sequencing and environment, not a CSS filter or category toggle.

## Remaining limitations

Encounter identities, names, quotations, exact years, and locations are not published until supplied or verified. Destination pages remain structural. The type system still uses platform stacks pending final licensing. Archive classification is an authored first selection, not a complete automated judgement of all 530 files.
