# Story 03 — Laos pack

## Editorial identity

- **Series:** THE JOURNEY — 03
- **Public title:** Laos
- **Status:** OWNER_REVIEW
- **Period:** approximately December 2024 to mid-January 2025
- **Centre:** Luang Prabang
- **Core:** staying long enough for travel to become normal life.

The story begins with the owner returning to Icon Klub for another Bloody Mary eleven years after his first. This time he has a job. Work at Laos Buffalo Dairy provides the structure; the Community House, ordinary errands, familiar roads, people and animals create the attachment. It ends with the departure towards Bangkok and the hope that Lana and Lao are doing well.

## Owner facts used

- Bus from Thailand to Luang Prabang; no invented Laos itinerary.
- Earlier Luang Prabang visit approximately eleven years before; first Bloody Mary at Icon Klub.
- Five to six weeks at Laos Buffalo Dairy, primarily giving tours; ice-cream service and ordinary help as needed.
- Preparation in Thailand over morning coffee; genuine nerves before the first tours; improvement tour by tour.
- Four workdays / three days off; morning pickup at the Community House; shared or simply arranged dinners.
- Community House life: Beerlao, music, stories, one indirect smoke joke, Lao the kitten, and Pauline.
- Free days: gym, groceries, scooter, food, river, photography, sunsets and familiar routes.
- Lana was the favourite buffalo. Lao was the kitten. Both carry the goodbye.
- Train back towards Bangkok; exact transfer mechanics deliberately omitted.
