# People System

## Purpose

People is about encounters, not portrait inventory. The unit of publication is a moment of attention: a task, exchange, gesture, movement, or face within its environment.

## Entry model

- photograph ID;
- display name, first name, or anonymous label when appropriate;
- consent/publication status;
- verified public place and year when known;
- one-line memory;
- short encounter text or quotation when supplied;
- related journey, destination, story, and collection IDs;
- alt text describing visible content without invented identity;
- privacy notes kept outside public output.

Unknown values remain `null`. Names, relationships, feelings, and quotations must never be inferred from appearance.

## Current implementation

`/people/` opens with `P1260248.jpg`, then sequences `P1260635.jpg`, `P1190682 Kopie.jpg`, and `IMG_9906.jpg`. Captions state only visible action or “place unconfirmed.” The page links into Black & White as a related photographic discovery.

## Editorial rules

Do not crop faces carelessly, reduce people to exotic detail, or publish private context. Prefer action and environmental context over a wall of headshots. One strong encounter is more valuable than twenty unedited portraits.
