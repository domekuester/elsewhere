# Malaysia — six questions

The story is written and it holds up as it is. Nothing below is missing information that had to be
invented in the meantime — nothing has been invented anywhere.

Two of these are blocking: without them the photographs cannot be captioned with a city, because not a
single Malaysia photograph in the archive carries one. The other four would make the next draft better,
not possible.

---

**1. Which city is which? (blocking)**
The city photographs fall in two blocks: 29 Sep – 8 Oct 2024, and 17–21 Apr 2025. Is all of that Kuala
Lumpur, or is George Town in there too? The mural of the child at the window (17 Apr), the old clock
tower (21 Apr) and the flower shop (18 Apr) are the three frames that look like a different, lower city
to me — but looking is not knowing, so they stay uncaptioned until you say.

**2. Which of the island photographs are Kapas? (blocking)**
There are two island blocks — 20–30 Aug 2024, and 23 Apr – 2 May 2025. Both, one, or neither?

**3. How did you actually end up on Kapas in 2016?**
You said by accident. That word is doing a lot of work. Two or three sentences about how it happened
would be the strongest passage in the chapter, and right now it is one line.

**4. What does an ordinary day on Kapas look like?**
Not the highlights. When you wake up, what you do with the day, when the light changes, what the noise
level is. The photographs show hammocks, an empty volleyball net, a rooster, water at four different
times of day. They are missing the day they belong to.

**5. Your friends — do you want them in the story at all?**
The chapter currently says only that you have met friends there since 2014 and that you stop arriving
as a stranger. No names are needed, ever. But if there is a habit — the same place, the same first
evening, the same thing you always do first — that would carry the return better than any sentence
about returning.

**6. Was there one frame on this trip that changed how you think about photography?**
Two candidates from the outside: the magenta close-up of the curling frond (5 Oct 2024), which nobody
takes while passing through, and the portrait with the red boxing gloves (30 Apr 2025). If it was
neither, which one was it?
