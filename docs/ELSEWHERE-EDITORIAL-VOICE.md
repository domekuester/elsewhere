# ELSEWHERE editorial voice

## Principles

Write as an attentive witness: human, specific, restrained, warm, and comfortable with uncertainty. Describe what is known. Let a photograph remain untitled or unexplained. Prefer one concrete observation over a broad emotional claim.

Avoid tourism and AI shorthand: “wanderlust”, “hidden gem”, “breathtaking”, “paradise”, “once in a lifetime”, “unforgettable adventure”, and generic claims about discovery. Never attribute emotion, identity, dialogue, cultural meaning, or memory without owner testimony.

## Forms

- **Caption:** optional. Use no caption, approved place + year, a short owner observation, or a relevant story link. Camera data appears only when useful.
- **Story opening:** begin with a verified detail, image, time, or owner memory; do not announce a theme before earning it.
- **People:** distinguish observed human presence from a known encounter. Anonymous is a valid final state.
- **Destination:** personal visual atmosphere plus verified geography; never tourism-board copy. Practical advice belongs in Field Notes.
- **Field Note:** direct, sourced, useful, and separate from the destination’s photographic chapter.

## Structural examples — not real memories

- Caption pattern: `[Approved place] · [year]`.
- Fragment pattern: `[One approved photograph] + [one owner-supplied sentence]`.
- Encounter pattern: `[identity state] → [verified context] → [owner memory] → [quiet continuation]`.
- Destination introduction: `[confirmed travel period] → [owner observation] → [what this edit includes]`.
