## GENERAL ARCHIVE — new hero

**Selected:** `photo-0182` · P1230662.jpg · a field of churning blue-white water, edge to edge
**Status:** `AUTO_SELECTED` · owner approval **pending**
**Config:** `data/surface-heroes.json` → `surfaces.archive`

The archive is the deep body of work, and this opens it with something that is all depth and no
address: a full band of moving water, no horizon, no landmark, no place claimed. It is a Japan frame by
provenance and says so in its metadata, but it does not announce a country, which is exactly right for a
surface that is not a chapter. Vast tonal room for the title, and it repeats neither a chapter hero nor
the Home opening.

**This selection changed after rendering.** The first pick was `photo-0183` — the same water with two
silhouetted figures watching it, chosen precisely *for* the figures. In the real 72svh band it failed
both ways: at a centred focal the figures are cropped out entirely, so the reason for choosing it
disappears, and at a low focal the heads become enormous and the title sits on one of them. Rather than
keep a photograph whose composition the band could not show, the hero became the frame that *is* the
composition the band shows. This is what "render before deciding" is for.

| Alternative | Note |
| --- | --- |
| `photo-0183` | The same water with two watching figures. Better photograph, worse opening at this height. |
| `photo-0677` | NEW · a vast red sun over the sea. Beautiful, but reads as a destination sunset. |
| `photo-0600` | A fishing boat far out under a wide sky. Quieter; strong second choice. |
