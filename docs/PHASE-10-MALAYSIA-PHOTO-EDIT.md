# Phase 10 — Malaysia story photo edit

**Revised in Phase 10.1 and 10.3.** 22 photographs from a pool of 59. Roles are internal: they shape
the order and are never rendered.

Phase 10.1 changed three things:

- `photo-0040` **removed** — the owner confirmed the frame is Thailand, not Malaysia. It was the
  closer.
- `photo-0057` **added** as the new closer.
- `photo-0688` and `photo-0689` **added** — two of three newly ingested, owner-confirmed George
  Town frames. The George Town section is no longer text only.

Phase 10.3 changed one thing, and it was caused by the writing rather than by the photographs. The
Kapas section grew from two paragraphs to eleven, and the picture edit had to answer that:

- `photo-0108` **removed** — a cloud bank with the sun setting under it. The fourth
  cloud-over-water frame in a chapter that already had three, and the weakest of them.
- `photo-0059` **added** — a heavy grey cloud bank over dark open water, placed directly after *And
  it rains. Properly.* The storm paragraph had no photograph; this one is the only frame in the
  Malaysia pool that reads as weather rather than as good light, and it is the first landscape frame
  anywhere in the story's reading column, which gives the long Kapas run a beat it did not have.

Net count unchanged.

## The George Town frames

Three files arrived from the owner as `Georgtown.jpg`, `Georgtown 01.jpg` and `Georgtown 02.jpg`,
ingested as `photo-0689`, `photo-0687` and `photo-0688`. All three: 3024 × 4032, iPhone 12 mini,
4 October 2024, no GPS. Three frames from one morning — 07:41, 08:06 and 09:26 — which reads as a
walk.

They are the **only photographs on the site with a confirmed city**. Every other frame in the
archive carries `place: null`, because the owner's travel timeline knows countries and not cities.

## Decisions worth arguing with

**The opener is not the destination hero.** `photo-0060` (the skyline) is Malaysia's chapter hero and
`photo-0036` (storm light on a beach) is on Home. Neither opens the story. 0060 is used inside it,
once, as the establishing frame — that is intentional reuse in a different role, not the same picture
doing the same job twice.

**George Town arrives before it is named.** `photo-0688` sits one block above the chapter marker, so
the reader is somewhere else for a frame before being told where. That inversion is only honest now
that a photograph can prove the city; until Phase 10.1 the section was text only, because the frames
that *looked* like a lower old-town street (`photo-0075`, `photo-0093`, `photo-0088`) were a guess,
and a guess must not become a location. Those three are still uncaptioned and still out of the story.

**One caption, on `photo-0689`.** Twenty-two photographs and one caption. It reads *George Town,
Penang · 2024*, and it is the first time this site has been able to say where a photograph was taken.

**No portraits.** Nine Malaysia frames contain identifiable people: `photo-0039`, `photo-0076`,
`photo-0078`, `photo-0080`, `photo-0085`, `photo-0086`, `photo-0145`, `photo-0547`, `photo-0665`.
Every one of them is `storyCandidate: false` and `NEEDS_OWNER_INPUT` in `data/people-review.json`.
Public in an archive grid is not the same permission as featured in a story. The build refuses to
render any of them, by check, not by memory. This costs the edit real material —
`photo-0078` (the street-food stall) was the natural food frame and `photo-0039` (a man working a
drinks stall) was the best human frame in the city — and it is still the right call until the owner
says otherwise. See the owner questions.

**The closer changed for a reason that no longer exists.** `photo-0040` held that slot because it was
the last Malaysia frame before the journey moved on to Thailand. The owner has confirmed the frame is
Thailand, so that reasoning was never true. `photo-0057` was chosen for what it shows instead: a boat
pulled up on the sand, a jetty running out, and the light going. It is from 22 August 2024 — the day
after the opener — so the story now closes a few hundred metres from where it began. The rhyme is
thematic, not visual: the opener is a dark corridor of columns looking out to sea, the closer an open
beach under sunbeams.

**Chronology is broken twice, deliberately.** George Town (October 2024) sits between two blocks of
April 2025, and the closer is August 2024. The story is explicitly about a country visited three times
across two years and never claims to be one continuous trip, so narrative order beats capture order in
both places.

**October 2024 was not one city.** The George Town frames are 4 October; `photo-0060`, the Kuala
Lumpur skyline, is 29 September. That settles part of an open owner question: the 29 Sep – 8 Oct 2024
block contains at least two cities, so it cannot be captioned wholesale as either.

**Portrait-heavy by nature.** Every column frame in the story is portrait and every full-bleed frame
is landscape, because that is how the pool falls. The rhythm that comes out of it — edge-to-edge
landscape, then a tall frame in the reading column — is a consequence turned into a structure rather
than a design imposed on the material. The two George Town frames are 3:4 and sit in the column with
everything else.

## Alt text

Written for this story and stored with it in `src/data/journey.ts`. The catalog carries none for
Malaysia, so these are the only written description of these frames that exists, and they also travel
into the page's `ImageObject` schema as captions. They describe what is visible. They name no city —
including the two George Town frames, whose confirmed location is carried by the caption and the
schema's `contentLocation` (*George Town, Penang, Malaysia*) rather than stuffed into alt text a
screen reader has to sit through.
