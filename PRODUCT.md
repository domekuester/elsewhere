# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visually curious travellers, photographers, editors, and readers who arrive through a destination, story, person, or image and want to continue exploring through connected visual narratives.

## Product Purpose

ELSEWHERE is an independent travel-photography publication connecting places, people, and moments. Success means the photography is remembered, exploration feels irresistible, and a large archive remains coherent without becoming a chronological blog or portfolio grid.

## Brand Personality

Elemental, attentive, intimate. Calm enough to let photographs breathe; confident enough to create memorable scale and rhythm.

## Anti-references

Generic travel blogs, influencer feeds, stock-tourism portals, Instagram grids, photography portfolio templates, card-heavy SaaS layouts, and decorative “luxury” interfaces that sacrifice readability.

## Design Principles

- Let every major decision strengthen the photography.
- Sequence rather than stack: the page should feel edited like a book or film.
- Make relationships between themes, places, and stories discoverable without exposing the archive as a database.
- Keep the interface quiet, legible, responsive, and fast.
- Treat provisional geography and personal location data honestly and privately.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Semantic navigation, keyboard access, visible focus, descriptive alternatives, resilient native scrolling, and a first-class reduced-motion mode are required. Mobile is an authored experience, not a reduced desktop layout.
